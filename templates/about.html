ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About Unsolved Metrics

Where intelligence meets the unknown.

This is a customer churn prediction project built on a Netflix customer dataset. It uses exploratory data analysis to uncover patterns in customer behaviour, and an XGBoost machine learning model to predict which subscribers are likely to cancel. The app shows who is leaving, the early warning signs to watch for, and how much revenue is at risk.

OVERVIEW

The business problem

Netflix earns its revenue from monthly subscriptions, so every cancellation is recurring revenue lost. Winning a new subscriber costs far more than keeping an existing one, yet unhappy customers are usually only noticed once they have already left. This project explores 5,000 customer records to understand churn before it happens.

METHODOLOGY

From raw data to a working web application

An end-to-end workflow covering data preparation, statistical analysis, machine learning and a web application.

STAGEAPPROACHTOOLS
1 · Data preparation & feature engineering Audited and cleansed the raw customer data (5,000 records, 14 columns), handling 2,100 missing values across 10 columns and removing a blank row. For the exploratory analysis, engineered 15 calculated fields across churn and revenue, engagement, customer profile, and risk and segmentation. PythonpandasNumPy
2 · Statistical analysis Investigated 15 business questions across customer behaviour, payment patterns and revenue exposure, validating key findings with an independent two-sample t-test and chi-square tests of independence. pandasNumPySciPy
3 · Feature selection Built the model from the original columns, one-hot encoded into 18 candidate features. Chi-square scoring (SelectKBest) kept the 7 most strongly associated with churn, keeping the model focused on the strongest signals. scikit-learn
4 · Model development Benchmarked 10 classification algorithms under identical conditions (70 / 30 train-test split, fixed random state). XGBoost delivered the strongest results on unseen data: 96.4% accuracy, 97.4% precision and 95.3% recall. scikit-learnXGBoostCatBoost
5 · Web application Delivered the analysis and trained model as a Flask web application, giving stakeholders direct access to the data, insights and churn predictions. FlaskHTMLCSS
